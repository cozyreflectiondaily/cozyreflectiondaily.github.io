VISUAL IDEA
The top begins warmer, more tactile, and more nostalgic.
As the reader moves downward, the palette and surfaces become quieter,
cleaner, and slightly cooler, representing movement from memory toward the present.

The five SVG sketches are intentionally restrained hand-drawn support images,
not dominant illustrations. The writing remains the primary focus.
